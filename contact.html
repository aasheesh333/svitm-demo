Contact FAQ

Quick answers.

How quickly will I get a reply?

Enquiries are answered within one working day. Admissions calls are usually returned the same day if you leave a message before 4 pm.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Yes, for a general campus tour between 10 am and 5 pm, Monday to Saturday. For a programme-specific meeting or a group visit, call ahead so the right faculty member is available.

Do you have a WhatsApp number?

Admissions can be reached on WhatsApp at +91 93155 00924 during office hours. Please avoid sending documents over WhatsApp; use the applicant portal instead.

Where do I send a grievance?

Write to info@svivekananditm.com with the subject “Grievance” and include your roll number or application reference. The registrar's office acknowledges grievances within three working days.

One last thing

Ready to apply instead?

The application takes about eight minutes and needs no documents. A counsellor calls you within one working day.
