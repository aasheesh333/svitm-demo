Our story

Built by educators. Shaped by employers.

Swami Vivekanand Institute of Technology & Management is a registered trade-mark division of the Swami Vivekanand Educational Society, registered with the Government of Haryana (Regd. No. 02766) under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

The society began with a straightforward observation: too many graduates held certificates that employers did not trust. So SVITM was built around a different question — what should a graduate be able to do on their first day at work?

That question still shapes everything: the curriculum, the lab equipment, who teaches, and how we measure success. We work with employers to design modules, place students on live projects and review outcomes each semester.

Our vision

To be the most trusted skills-first institute in North India — where a qualification is proof of capability, and where learners from any background can build a career they are proud of.

  • Teaching that is judged by learner outcomes
  • Access for first-generation learners
  • Employer trust built on real work

Our mission

To deliver industry-aligned degrees, diplomas and certificates through small batches, practising faculty and structured placement support — across every centre we operate.

  • Curriculum reviewed with industry each year
  • One mentor for every 30 learners
  • Career support from semester one
