Admissions FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Still unsure about something? Call the helpline or send an email — we reply to every enquiry.

Ask a question
Is there an application fee?

This demo application is free and does not send data anywhere. For the real 2026–27 intake, the online application fee is a one-time ₹500–₹1,000 depending on the programme (illustrative demo figure) and is non-refundable. It is adjusted against your first instalment if you enrol.

Which scholarships can I apply for?

You can be considered for a merit scholarship of up to 30% of tuition based on Class 12 or degree results, a Haryana domicile concession, and a ₹5,000 early-bird waiver if you confirm your seat by 30 June 2026. Scholarships are normally combined subject to a cap, so ask admissions for a written calculation before paying.

Can working professionals study here?

Yes. MBA, BBA, BCA and Certificate in Digital Marketing & AI offer online and hybrid modes with evening or weekend batches at select centres. You will need to attend labs and examinations in person where the programme requires it. A counsellor can map the timetable to your work schedule before you apply.

Do I need to upload documents now?

No. This demo form does not accept uploads. In the live process, you upload scanned marksheets and ID proof in the applicant portal after submitting the form (PDF or JPG under 2 MB each). Originals are then checked at document verification between 20 and 28 July 2026. Demo dates apply.

Can I transfer to another SVITM centre?

Centre transfers are possible subject to seat availability in the receiving centre, the same programme and mode, and any fee difference. Submit a written request before the semester begins; one transfer is normally permitted during the programme. Admission approval is required and cannot be guaranteed.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds follow the applicable UGC and Haryana government fee norms. As a guide: full refund of tuition (less the application fee) if you withdraw before classes begin; partial refund during the first two weeks; no tuition refund after that. Hostel and transport charges follow separate terms. This is an illustrative demo summary — your offer letter carries the binding policy.
