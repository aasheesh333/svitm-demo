Course FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

Anything else, ask the admissions desk on +91 93155 00924.

Can I change my programme after applying?

Yes. You can change your programme or centre once before document verification, subject to seat availability and eligibility. Speak to your counsellor and they will update the application for you.

Are the degrees recognised?

Degree programmes run through university-affiliated pathways, and skill programmes are NSDC-aligned. This is a demo site — always confirm the current affiliation and approval status with the admissions office before paying any fee.

Do you offer weekend or online classes?

MBA, BBA, BCA and the Digital Marketing & AI certificate offer online or hybrid modes with evening and weekend batches at select centres. Labs and examinations may still require in-person attendance.

What scholarships are available?

Merit scholarships up to 30% of tuition, a Haryana domicile concession, and a ₹5,000 early-bird waiver for confirmed seats. Scholarships can usually be combined up to a cap — ask for a written fee calculation.

Is placement guaranteed?

No institute can honestly guarantee a job. What we provide is structured placement support: CV clinics, mock interviews, employer drives and a 40+ partner network. Outcomes depend on your performance and the market.
