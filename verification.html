Verification FAQ

Common questions.

My result is not showing. What should I do?

Check the roll number, programme and semester combination. Results appear within 21 days of the last exam. If a subject is marked pending, contact the examination cell with your admit card.

Can I download a marksheet?

In the live portal, a provisional marksheet can be downloaded once results are published. Official transcripts are issued by the examination cell after a written request and fee payment.

What if a certificate shows as withdrawn?

A withdrawn status means the certificate was replaced, corrected or cancelled. Ask the holder for the replacement certificate ID, or contact the examination cell to confirm the current record.

Can employers verify without the candidate present?

Yes. The certificate ID is enough to confirm the holder's name, programme, grade and validity. No personal contact details are shown.

Is there a fee for verification?

Online verification is free. A formal verification letter on institute letterhead, if required by an employer, may carry a small administrative fee.
