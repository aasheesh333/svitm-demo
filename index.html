Who we are

Education that keeps up with the world of work.

Swami Vivekanand Institute of Technology & Management is a registered educational society initiative built around one belief: a qualification should change what you can do, not just what you can say.

We combine degree pathways with short, employer-designed skill programmes — taught in small batches, with labs, live projects and placement support from day one.

  • Industry practitioners teach alongside full-time faculty
  • Paid internships and live client projects built into programmes
  • Flexible weekend and hybrid options for working learners
  • One counselling team from first enquiry to first job

The SVITM difference

Built for how people actually learn.

Small batches, working faculty and a career cell that stays with you — not a certificate mill.

Small batches, real attention

Maximum 30 learners per batch, with a named mentor for every student and monthly progress reviews.

Industry faculty

Practising engineers, marketers, lawyers and clinicians teach the modules they work in every day.

Placement from day one

CV clinics, mock interviews and a 40+ employer network, starting in your first semester — not your last.
