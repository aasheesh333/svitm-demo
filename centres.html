Centre FAQ

Before you visit.

Call the centre directly for batch timings and lab availability.

Are all programmes available at every centre?

No. Degree programmes run at the Sohna main campus and selected partner campuses. Skill and certificate batches vary by centre. Check the programme tags on each centre card or call before visiting.

Can I transfer between centres?

Yes, subject to seat availability in the same programme and mode, plus any fee difference. Submit a written request before the semester begins. One transfer is normally permitted.

Can I verify documents at any centre?

Original documents are verified at the centre where you enrol, or at the Sohna admissions office. Carry originals plus one set of photocopies; verification usually takes under 30 minutes.

Do centres run weekend batches?

Selected centres run Saturday and Sunday batches for MBA, BBA, BCA and digital-skills programmes. Weekend lab access depends on the centre's equipment and staffing.
